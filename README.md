## What I Build for · 我为什么开发

- Get ideas into people’s hands sooner.  
  让想法更快变成别人用得上的东西。
- Find faster ways to test research hypotheses.  
  更快找到办法验证研究中的猜想。
- Cut through the noise and make room to think about what matters.  
  减少信息噪音，把注意力留给真正值得思考的问题。
- Explore beyond what I already know and find new questions to ask.  
  走出熟悉的范围，在更大的世界里发现新的问题。

## How I Work · 我怎么做开发

In my personal projects, I focus on shaping the architecture and deciding what the tests need to verify. I work closely with AI on implementation and rarely review the generated code line by line.

做个人项目时，我主要把精力放在架构设计和明确测试目标上。实现时，我和 AI 密切协作，较少逐行审阅生成的代码。

## What I'm Working Toward · 我想实现什么

I'm exploring new ways to work with AI so I can try more ideas and see where they lead. I see these repositories as extensions of my imagination. I hope some of what starts here will one day become real.

我在探索和 AI 协作的新方式，想把更多想法动手试一遍，看看它们会发展成什么。我把这些仓库看作想象力的延伸，希望从这里开始的一些想法，有一天能成为现实。
